COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions our guests ask us before booking.

How do I begin customizing a package?

Simply fill out the custom enquiry form above with your basic preferences. One of our dedicated travel experts will review your request and contact you within 24 hours to schedule a detailed phone or video consultation to craft your perfect itinerary.

Are your itineraries fully customizable?

Yes, absolutely. Every aspect of your trip—from flights and luxury accommodations to private transfers and exclusive excursions—can be tailored to your specific taste, pace, and budget.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation policies vary depending on the specific hotels, airlines, and activities booked. However, we always prioritize booking flexible rates for our clients and will clearly outline all cancellation terms before you make any payment.

Do you provide travel insurance?

We highly recommend travel insurance. We partner with top-tier insurance providers and can seamlessly add a comprehensive policy to your package that covers cancellations, medical emergencies, and lost luggage.

What if I face an emergency during my trip?

You will have access to a dedicated 24/7 concierge line. Whether it's a missed flight, a medical issue, or a last-minute restaurant reservation, our global team is always awake and ready to assist you instantly.
